One line, under 200 characters: the most creative excuse a hardware wallet gives for refusing to sign a transaction at 3 AM. The wallet's seed phrase is a sleeping baby — wake it at 3 AM and it cries in twelve words, and all twelve of them are "no".

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_DIR` for a local run, e.g. `OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py`).

## The line

> My seed phrase is asleep. Wake it at 3 AM and it cries in twelve words — all twelve of them 'no'.

97 characters. No battery joke, no firmware update, no cold storage — the 3 AM hour itself is the joke, and the refusal is the punchline.
